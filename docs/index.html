News aggregator · not a plain RSS reader

One story per
real-world event.

NewsGator gathers every article covering the same event into one living story, keeps its neutral summary current as new facts arrive, gives it an honest, clickbait-free title, and sends you back to the original publisher when it is time to read more.

Two engines: native Apple apps powered by Apple Intelligence, or your own self-hosted NewsGator server.

How it works

Three steps from news flood to signal.

NewsGator is built for people who want coverage from many sources without reading the same story five times.

01

Add your sources

Bring in RSS feeds, IMAP newsletters, or a synced reader account such as FreshRSS, Miniflux, or Inoreader. OPML import keeps setup quick.

02

NewsGator groups the event

It fetches the full text, clusters every article about the same real-world event, writes a neutral summary, and assigns a factual headline.

03

You triage, then go deeper

Read a short story first, open the publisher when it matters, then come back later to ask your archive, share a story, or follow the distilled story RSS feed.

Two ways to run it

The same idea, two very different engines.

Pick the setup that fits your life: zero infrastructure on Apple devices, or a full self-hosted server for your household.

Standalone native apps

Apple Intelligence, no server

Native iPhone, iPad, and Mac apps powered by Apple Private Cloud Compute first, then the on-device model. Your library and read state sync through iCloud.

  • No server to run and no subscription required.
  • Available in 10 languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese.
  • Siri and Shortcuts support for faster capture and reading flows.
  • Listen to your stories, even in the car: natural on-device voices and CarPlay.
  • The same story model: one event, one evolving story.
Self-hosted server

Python, FastAPI, SvelteKit, your AI

Run the NewsGator server yourself and point it at any OpenAI-compatible AI you prefer: oMLX, Ollama, llama.cpp, LM Studio, or another compatible endpoint.

  • Multi-user reading with per-user feeds, read state, and preferences.
  • Web app + PWA, archive chat, story sharing, story RSS feed, and activity tracing.
  • RSS, IMAP newsletters, and FreshRSS/Miniflux/Inoreader-style reader sync.
